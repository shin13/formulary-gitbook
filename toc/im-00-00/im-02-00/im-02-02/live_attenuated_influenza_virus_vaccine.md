| Adverse Effects    | >10%: Gastrointestinal: Abdominal pain (children, adolescents: 2% to 12%), decreased appetite (children: 13% to 21%) Nervous system: Asthenia (adults: <=26%), fatigue (adults: <=26%), headache (children: 3% to 9%; adults: 40%), irritability (children: 12% to 21%), lethargy (children: 7% to 14%) Neuromuscular & skeletal: Myalgia (children: 2% to 6%; adults: 17%) Respiratory: Cough (adults: 14%), nasal congestion (children: <=58%; adults: 9%), pharyngitis (children: 5% to 11%; adults: 28%), rhinorrhea (children: <=58%; adults: 44%) Miscellaneous: Fever (children: 4% to 16%) 1% to 10%: Nervous system: Chills (children: 2% to 4%; adults: 9%), hypoactivity (children, adolescents: 6%) Respiratory: Sinusitis (adults: 4%), sneezing (children: 2%), wheezing (children: 6 to 23 months: 6%; 24 to 59 months: 2%) Frequency not defined: Respiratory: Exacerbation of asthma (children) Postmarketing (any population): Cardiovascular: Pericarditis, syncope Dermatologic: Skin rash Gastrointestinal: Diarrhea, nausea, vomiting Hypersensitivity: Hypersensitivity reaction (including anaphylaxis) Nervous system: Bell palsy, encephalitis (vaccine associated), Guillain-Barre syndrome, meningitis (including eosinophilic meningitis), subacute necrotizing encephalomyelopathy (Leigh syndrome exacerbation) Respiratory: Epistaxis |
