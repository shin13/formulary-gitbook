| Dosing             | Usually, for adults, 11.25 mg subcutaneously once every 12 weeks. [Prostate Cancer] Serum testosterone concentrations should be measured at least every 3 months during the first 6 months of treatment. [Endometriosis] The recommended duration of treatment with Leuplin Depot 3-Month 11.25 mg as monotherapy or in combination with norethindrone acetate is 6 months. If symptoms of endometriosis recur after a 6-month course of therapy, retreatment for an additional 6 months with Leuplin Depot in combination with norethindrone acetate 5 mg daily may be considered. Retreatment should not exceed 6 months. Prior to initiating retreatment, bone mineral density (BMD) assessment is recommended to confirm that BMD is within normal limits. Retreatment with Leuplin Depot monotherapy is not recommended. If the patient is unable to receive norethindrone acetate, retreatment is not recommended. Prior to initiating combination therapy with Leuplin Depot and norethindrone acetate, it is recommended to assess the patient's cardiovascular risk and address modifiable risk factors, such as cigarette smoking. [Uterine Leiomyomata (Fibroids)] one Leuplin Depot 3M injection. The symptoms associated with uterine leiomyomata will recur following discontinuation of therapy. If additional treatment with Leuplin Depot 3M is contemplated, bone density should be assessed prior to initiation of therapy to ensure that values are within normal limits. Recommended dose: a single injection. Symptoms associated with uterine fibroids may recur following discontinuation of therapy. If an additional course of treatment with Leuplin Depot 3-Month 11.25 mg is considered, the patient's BMD should be evaluated prior to initiating therapy to confirm that BMD is within normal limits. [Central Precocious Puberty] The dosage must be individualized. The recommended starting dose should be adjusted according to body weight: Children weighing 20 kg or more: The administered dose is 1 mL of Leuplin Depot 3-Month 11.25 mg as a single subcutaneous injection every 3 months. Children weighing less than 20 kg: In these rare cases, the following dose should be administered based on the clinical activity of central precocious puberty: A single subcutaneous injection of 0.5 mL (containing 5.625 mg of leuprolide acetate) of Leuplin Depot 3-Month 11.25 mg every 3 months. Any remaining suspension must be discarded. Children's weight gain should be monitored.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
