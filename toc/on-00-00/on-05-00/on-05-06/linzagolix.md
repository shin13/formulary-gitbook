# Linzagolix

## Yselty 100mg

| TAH Drug Code      | [OYSE](https://www.tahsda.org.tw/drugs/hissearch.php?drug_code=OYSE)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
|:-------------------|:---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Indications        | Uterine fibroids, moderate to severe: Treatment of moderate to severe symptoms of uterine fibroids in patients of reproductive age.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Dosing             | Uterine fibroids, moderate to severe: Combination with hormonal add-back therapy: Oral: Initial: 100 mg once daily in combination with estradiol and norethindrone acetate; may increase to 200 mg once daily if needed. Refer to manufacturers labeling for concomitant hormonal therapy dosing. --- Monotherapy, patients for whom concomitant hormonal therapy is not preferred/recommended: Oral: 100 mg once daily. --- Monotherapy, short-term use for reduction of uterine and fibroid volume: Oral: 200 mg once daily for a maximum duration of 6 months.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

| Contraindications  | Hypersensitivity to linzagolix or any component of the formulation; pregnancy; breastfeeding; osteoporosis; genital bleeding with no known cause.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
