| Indications        | Breast cancer, early, HER2-positive: - Adjuvant treatment (as a single agent) of HER2-positive (immunohistochemical [IHC] 3+ or positive in situ hybridization [ISH+]) breast cancer in adults with residual invasive disease following neoadjuvant trastuzumab (with or without pertuzumab) and taxane-based treatment. - Neoadjuvant treatment (followed by a taxane, trastuzumab, and pertuzumab [THP]) of stage II or III HER2-positive (IHC 3+ or ISH+) breast cancer (as determined by an authorized test) in adults. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Breast cancer, unresectable or metastatic, hormone receptor positive, HER2-low or HER2-ultralow: - Treatment (as a single agent) of unresectable or metastatic hormone receptor-positive, HER2-low (IHC 1+ or IHC 2+ and ISH-) or HER2-ultralow (IHC 0 with membrane staining) breast cancer (as determined by an authorized test) in adults with progression on ?1 endocrine therapies in the metastatic setting. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Breast cancer, unresectable or metastatic, HER2-low: - Treatment (as a single agent) of unresectable or metastatic HER2-low (IHC 1+ or IHC 2+ and ISH-) breast cancer (as determined by an authorized test) in adults who have received a prior chemotherapy in the metastatic setting or developed disease recurrence during or within 6 months of completing adjuvant chemotherapy. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Breast cancer, unresectable or metastatic, HER2-positive: - First-line treatment (in combination with pertuzumab) of unresectable or metastatic HER2-positive (IHC 3+ or ISH+) breast cancer (as determined by an authorized test) in adults. - Treatment (as a single agent) of unresectable or metastatic HER2-positive (IHC 3+ or ISH+) breast cancer in adults who have received a prior anti–HER2-based regimen either in the metastatic setting, or in the neoadjuvant or adjuvant setting and have developed disease recurrence during or within 6 months of completing therapy. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Gastric cancer, locally advanced or metastatic, HER2-positive: - Treatment (as a single agent) of locally advanced or metastatic HER2-positive (IHC 3+ or IHC 2+ and ISH+) gastric or gastroesophageal junction adenocarcinoma in adults who have received a prior trastuzumab-based regimen. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Non–small cell lung cancer, unresectable or metastatic, HER2 (ERBB2)-mutation positive: - Treatment (as a single agent) of unresectable or metastatic non–small cell lung cancer with activating HER2 (ERBB2) mutations (as detected by an authorized test) in adults who have received a prior systemic therapy. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Solid tumors, unresectable or metastatic, HER2-positive: - Treatment (as a single agent) of unresectable or metastatic HER2-positive (IHC 3+) solid tumors in adults who have received prior systemic treatment and have no satisfactory alternative treatment options. |
| Dosing             | 5.4 mg/kg given as an intravenous infusion once every 3 weeks (21-day cycle) until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. (Note: A different recommended dosage applies to gastric cancer.)
