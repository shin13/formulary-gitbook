| Dosing             | Colorectal cancer, advanced: IV: 85 mg/m2 on day 1 every 2 weeks (in combination with infusional fluorouracil/leucovorin) until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. Colon cancer, stage III, adjuvant therapy: IV: 85 mg/m2 on day 1 every 2 weeks (in combination with infusional fluorouracil/leucovorin) for up to 12 cycles. Gastric cancer (off-label use): Adjuvant therapy: IV: 130 mg/m2 on day 1 of a 21-day treatment cycle (in combination with capecitabine) for 8 cycles following D2 gastrectomy in patients who did not receive preoperative therapy. Pancreatic cancer, advanced or metastatic (off-label use): FOLFIRINOX regimen: IV: 85 mg/m2 every 2 weeks (in combination with fluorouracil, leucovorin, and irinotecan) for up to 6 months.
