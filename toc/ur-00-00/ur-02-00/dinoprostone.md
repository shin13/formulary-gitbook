| Indications        | Cervical ripening                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Dosing             | Vaginal: Insert 3 mg (one tablet) high into the posterior fornix of the vagina; may give one additional dose of 3 mg 6 to 8 hours later if needed (maximum: 6 mg/24 hours).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Hepatic Impairment | Dose adjustment not necessary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Renal Impairment   | Dose adjustment not necessary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Contraindications  | Hypersensitivity to prostaglandins or any component of the formulation. Active cardiac, pulmonary, renal, or hepatic disease; history of caesarean section or major uterine surgery; cephalopelvic disproportion; fetal malpresentation; fetal distress; history of difficult labor/traumatic delivery; ?6 previous term pregnancies; placenta previa or unexplained vaginal bleeding during this pregnancy; ruptured membranes; history of or existing pelvic inflammatory disease unless adequately treated.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Adverse Effects    | 1% to 10%: Genitourinary: Hypertonic uterine contractions (with and without fetal distress: 2% to 5%), uterine contractions (abnormal: 7%), vaginal discomfort (warm feeling: 2%) Neuromuscular & skeletal: Back pain (3%) <1%: Gastrointestinal: Abdominal pain, diarrhea, nausea, vomiting Miscellaneous: Fever Postmarketing: Cardiovascular: Acute myocardial infarction (with history of prior myocardial infarction), embolism (amniotic fluid [anaphylactoid syndrome of pregnancy]), hypotension Genitourinary: Premature rupture of membranes, uterine rupture Hematologic & oncologic: Disseminated intravascular coagulation (postpartum) Hypersensitivity: Anaphylactic shock, anaphylaxis, angioedema, hypersensitivity reaction, nonimmune anaphylaxis Nervous system: Headache |
