# Estriol

## Esvatin vaginal tablet 0.5mg

| TAH Drug Code      | [EESV](https://www.tahsda.org.tw/drugs/hissearch.php?drug_code=EESV)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
|:-------------------|:------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Indications        | Diagnostic aid Vaginal surgery Vulvar and vaginal atrophy associated with menopause                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Dosing             | Diagnostic aid: Intravaginal: Insert 1 suppository/pessary daily for 7 days prior to reevaluating the cytology. Vaginal surgery (perioperative): Intravaginal: Insert 1 suppository/pessary (0.5 mg) daily beginning 2 weeks prior to the operation. Vulvar and vaginal atrophy associated with menopause: Intravaginal: Initial: Insert 1 suppository/pessary (0.5 mg) once daily for 2 to 3 weeks; maintenance: Insert 1 suppository/pessary once to twice weekly. Discontinue every 2 to 3 months for a period of 4 weeks to assess the necessity for further treatment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Hepatic Impairment | Dose adjustment not necessary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Renal Impairment   | Dose adjustment not necessary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Contraindications  | Hypersensitivity to estriol or to any component of the formulation; active or history of arterial thromboembolic disease (eg, stroke, myocardial infarction) or thrombophlebitis; breast cancer (known, suspected, or history of); estrogen-dependent tumor (known or suspected); known or history of thrombophilic disorders (eg, protein C, protein S, antithrombin deficiency); porphyria; pregnancy; DVT or PE (current or history of); hepatic impairment or active disease; undiagnosed abnormal genital bleeding; untreated endometrial hyperplasia.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Adverse Effects    | 1% to 10%: Dermatologic: Pruritus Genitourinary: Genital pruritus, mastalgia (including discomfort) Local: Application-site pruritus <1%: Dermatologic: Prurigo Genitourinary: Genital rash, pelvic pain Infection: Candidiasis Local: Application-site irritation (including localized warm feeling) Nervous system: Headache Frequency not defined: Cardiovascular: Palpitations Endocrine & metabolic: Premenstrual syndrome (tension) Gastrointestinal: Lower abdominal pain Genitourinary: Cystitis, urinary frequency, vaginal discharge Nervous system: Depression Neuromuscular & skeletal: Limb pain (leg) Postmarketing: Endocrine & metabolic: Fluid retention, postmenopausal bleeding (spotting) Gastrointestinal: Nausea Genitourinary: Change in cervical secretions (discharge), dysuria, genital pain (burning sensation), vulvovaginal burning Respiratory: Flu-like symptoms |
