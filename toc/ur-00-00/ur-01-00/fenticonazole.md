# Fenticonazole

## Lomexin vaginal capsule 200mg

| TAH Drug Code      | [ELOM](https://www.tahsda.org.tw/drugs/hissearch.php?drug_code=ELOM)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
|:-------------------|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Indications        | Candidiasis, vulvovaginal Superficial fungal infection                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Dosing             | Candidiasis, vulvovaginal: Intravaginal: Capsule, 200 mg: Insert one 200 mg capsule at bedtime for 3 days.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Hepatic Impairment | Dose adjustment not necessary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Renal Impairment   | Dose adjustment not necessary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Contraindications  | Hypersensitivity to fenticonazole, other imidazole derivatives, or any component of the formulation; allergy to soya or peanut (contains soya lecithin [capsule formulation only]).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Adverse Effects    | <1%: Cardiovascular: Edema Dermatologic: Eczema, erythema of skin, exfoliation of skin, pruritus, skin irritation, skin rash Genitourinary: Vulvovaginal burning (vaginal application) Local: Application-site burning Frequency not defined: Hypersensitivity: Hypersensitivity reaction Local: Application-site erythema, local hypersensitivity reaction (application site) Postmarketing: Dermatologic: Contact dermatitis, erythematous rash |
