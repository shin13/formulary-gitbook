| Dosing             | Atrioventricular block, symptomatic and/or hemodynamically unstable: Continuous infusion: IV: Usual dosage range: 1 to 20 mcg/minute; initiate at the low end of the dosage range and titrate to clinical response. [2013.03.21版本FDA仿單] Recommended dosage for adults with heart block, Adams-Stokes attacks, and cardiac arrest: ●Bolus intravenous injection: Initial dose: 0.02mg to 0.06mg; subsequent dose range: 0.01mg to 0.2mg. ●Intramuscular: Use solution undiluted: Initial dose: 0.2mg; subsequent dose range: 0.02mg to 1mg. ●Subcutaneous: Use solution undiluted: Initial dose: 0.2mg; subsequent dose range: 0.15mg to 0.2mg. ●Intracardiac: Use solution undiluted: Initial dose: 0.02mg. Recommended dosage for adults with shock and hypoperfusion states: Intravenous infusion: Dilute 5mL (1mg) in 500 mL of 5% Dextrose Injection, 0.5mcg to 5mcg per minute. Recommended dosage for adults with bronchospasm occurring during anesthesia: Bolus intravenous injection: Dilute 1mL (0.2mg) in 9mL of Sodium Chloride Injection, or 5% Dextrose Injection. Initial dose: 0.01mg to 0.02mg; subsequent dose range: The initial dose may be repeated when necessary. |
