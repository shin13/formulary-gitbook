| Contraindications  | Hypersensitivity to amiloride, hydrochlorothiazide, any component of the formulation, or sulfonamide-derived drugs; presence of elevated serum potassium levels (>5.5 mEq/L); if patient is receiving other potassium-sparing agents (eg, spironolactone, triamterene), potassium-containing salt substitutes, potassium-rich diet, or potassium supplements (except in cases of severe and/or refractory hypokalemia); anuria; acute or chronic renal insufficiency; evidence of diabetic nephropathy. Patients with evidence of renal impairment (serum creatinine >1.5 mg/dL, BUN >30 mg/dL) or diabetes mellitus should not receive this medicine without close, frequent monitoring of serum electrolytes and renal function.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
