# Pravastatin sodium + Fenofibrate

## Pravafen 40/160mg

| TAH Drug Code      | [OPRAV](https://www.tahsda.org.tw/drugs/hissearch.php?drug_code=OPRAV)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
|:-------------------|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Indications        | [仿單] Indicated as an adjunct to diet and other non-pharmacological treatments (e.g., exercise, weight reduction) for the treatment of adult patients with mixed hyperlipidemia at high cardiovascular risk, to reduce triglycerides and increase HDL cholesterol when their LDL-C is adequately controlled with pravastatin 40 mg monotherapy.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Dosing             | [仿單] 建議劑量為每日服用一顆膠囊。並且繼續維持開始藥物治療前的飲食控制。 需以血清脂質濃度值監測病人對治療的反應。開始使用 Pravafen 後,通常血脂會快速下降,若 3 個月內無法獲得充足的反應,則應停止用藥。                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Hepatic Impairment | Dose adjustment required                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Renal Impairment   | Dose adjustment required                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Contraindications  | -Hypersensitivity to pravastatin, fenofibrate or fenofibric acid or any component of the formulation; active liver disease or decompensated cirrhosis, including unexplained, persistent liver function abnormality; severe kidney impairment or end-stage kidney disease, including those receiving dialysis; preexisting gallbladder disease. -Pregnancy and lactation. -Diabetes mellitus, Myopathy/rhabdomyolysis, Myasthenia gravis, Hematologic effects, Pancreatitis, Venous thromboembolism                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Adverse Effects    | >10%: Hepatic: Increased serum alanine aminotransferase (>=3 x ULN: <=13%), increased serum aspartate aminotransferase (>=3 x ULN: <=13%) Neuromuscular & skeletal: Musculoskeletal pain (25%) Respiratory: Upper respiratory tract infection (21%) 1% to 10%: Cardiovascular: Pulmonary embolism (1%) Dermatologic: Skin rash (1%), urticaria (1%) Gastrointestinal: Abdominal pain (5%), constipation (2%), diarrhea (>=3%), dyspepsia (>=3%) Nervous system: Dizziness (>=3%), pain (>=3%) Neuromuscular & skeletal: Arthralgia (>=3%), increased creatine phosphokinase in blood specimen (3%), limb pain (>=3%), myalgia (>=3%) Respiratory: Nasopharyngitis (>=3%), rhinitis (2%), sinusitis (>=3%), upper respiratory tract infection (>=3%), Respiratory: Cough (8%) <1%: Renal: Increased serum creatinine Neuromuscular & skeletal: Myopathy |
