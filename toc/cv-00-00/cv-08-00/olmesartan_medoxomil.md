# Olmesartan medoxomil

## Olmesardin 40mg

| TAH Drug Code      | [OOLM4](https://www.tahsda.org.tw/drugs/hissearch.php?drug_code=OOLM4)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
|:-------------------|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Indications        | Acute coronary syndrome Hypertension, chronic Proteinuric chronic kidney disease, diabetic or nondiabetic                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Dosing             | Acute coronary syndrome: Oral: Initial: 20 mg once daily; increase dose as tolerated up to 40 mg/day under close monitoring to avoid hypotension. Hypertension, chronic: Oral: 20 mg once daily; evaluate response monthly and titrate dose (eg, increase the daily dose by doubling) as needed up to 40 mg once daily; if additional blood pressure control is needed, consider combination therapy. Patients with severe asymptomatic hypertension and no signs of acute end organ damage should be evaluated for medication titration within 1 week.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Hepatic Impairment | Dose adjustment not necessary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Renal Impairment   | Dose adjustment required                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Contraindications  | Hypersensitivity to olmesartan or any component of the formulation; concomitant use with aliskiren in patients with moderate to severe renal impairment (GFR <60 mL/minute/1.73 m2).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Adverse Effects    | 1% to 10%: Nervous system: Dizziness (3%) <1%: Hypersensitivity: Facial edema Postmarketing: Dermatologic: Alopecia, maculopapular rash (Bhushan 2013), pruritus, urticaria Endocrine & metabolic: Hyperkalemia Gastrointestinal: Abdominal pain (Kang 2022), diarrhea (Onteddu 2018), gastritis (Shenbagaraj 2018), lymphocytic colitis (Djembissi Fotso 2023), nausea (Onteddu 2018), sprue-like symptoms (Aoyama 2022), vomiting (Onteddu 2018) Hepatic: Hepatitis (Rubin de Celix 2021), increased liver enzymes (Rubin de Celix 2021) Hypersensitivity: Anaphylaxis, angioedema, hypersensitivity angiitis (Vanbelleghem 2023) Nervous system: Asthenia Neuromuscular & skeletal: Rhabdomyolysis, subacute cutaneous lupus erythematosus (Gray 2019) Renal: Acute kidney injury (Kang 2022), increased serum creatinine |
