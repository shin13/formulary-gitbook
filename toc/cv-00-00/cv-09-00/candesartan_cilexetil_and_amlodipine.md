| Contraindications  | Hypersensitivity to candesartan, amlodipine, other dihydropyridines, or any component of the formulation; severe liver impairment, biliary cirrhosis, or cholestasis; severe hypotension; shock; outflow tract obstruction of the left ventricle (eg, hypertrophic obstructive cardiomyopathy, high-grade aortic stenosis); unstable heart failure following acute myocardial infarction; concomitant use with aliskiren-containing products in patients with diabetes mellitus or GFR <60 mL/minute/1.73m2.
