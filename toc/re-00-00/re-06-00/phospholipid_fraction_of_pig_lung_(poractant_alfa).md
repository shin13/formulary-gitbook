# Phospholipid fraction of pig lung (Poractant Alfa)

## Curosurf 120mg/1.5mL suspension

| TAH Drug Code      | [LCUROS](https://www.tahsda.org.tw/drugs/hissearch.php?drug_code=LCUROS)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|:-------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Indications        | Prevention & treatment of respiratory distress syndrome in premature infants.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Dosing             | 治療性用法: 建議在診斷為RDS後,儘速開始治療。建議劑量為一劑投予100到200毫克/公斤(1.25到2.5毫升/公斤)體重的藥劑。如果RDS仍然是嬰兒呼吸狀態持續或惡化的原因時,約每12小時,可額外再投予100毫克/公斤體重的劑量(doses) (最高總量為300-400毫克/公斤)。 預防性用法：出生後,有RDS風險或有介面活性劑缺乏證據的早產嬰兒需插氣管內管來使呼吸狀態穩定時,儘速(15分鐘內)給予一劑100到200毫克/公斤的藥劑。 投予第一劑後 6到12小時,可再投予100 毫克/公斤的劑量,然後,如果RDS仍是嬰兒呼吸狀態持續或惡化的原因時,12小時後可再給100 毫克/公斤的劑量 (最大總量為300-400 mg/kg)。 |
| Hepatic Impairment | Dose adjustment not necessary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Renal Impairment   | Dose adjustment not necessary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Contraindications  | Contraindicated in individuals allergic to the active ingredient or any of the excipients.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Adverse Effects    | Cardiovascular: Patent ductus arteriosus (60%), bradycardia, hypotension Hematologic & oncologic: Oxygen desaturation Miscellaneous: Obstruction of endotracheal tube <1%, postmarketing, and/or case reports: Pulmonary hemorrhage                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Pregnancy          | Unknown                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Lactation          | Unknown                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
