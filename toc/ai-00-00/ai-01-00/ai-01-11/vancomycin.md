| Dosing             | Adult usual dosage range: IV: Intermittent infusion: 15 to 20 mg/kg/dose (rounded to the nearest 250 mg) every 8 to 12 hours initially. Loading dose: Seriously ill patients with documented/suspected MRSA infection: A loading dose of 20 to 35 mg/kg (based on actual body weight; maximum: 3 g/dose). Continuous infusion: Loading dose: 15 to 20 mg/kg, followed by a maintenance continuous infusion dose of 30 to 40 mg/kg/day (up to 60 mg/kg/day). Pediatric general dosing: Infants, Children, and Adolescents: IV: Initial: 45 to 60 mg/kg/day divided every 6 to 8 hours; dose and frequency should be individualized based on serum concentrations. Neonatal general dosing: <29 weeks: 15 mg/kg/dose every 24 hours 29 to 35 weeks: 15 mg/kg/dose every 12 hours >35 weeks: 15 mg/kg/dose every 8 hours                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
