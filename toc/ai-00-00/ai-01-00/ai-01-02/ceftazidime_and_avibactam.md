| Dosing             | Note: Dosage recommendations are expressed as total grams of the ceftazidime/avibactam combination. (ceftazidime/avibactam sodium=4:1) Adult: ●Intra-abdominal infection: IV: 2.5 gm every 8 hours in combination with metronidazole. Total duration of therapy is 4 to 5 days. ●Metallo-beta-lactamase–producing Enterobacterales and multidrug-resistant Stenotrophomonas maltophilia infections: IV: 2.5 gm every 8 hours infused over 3 hours; use in combination with aztreonam. ●Pneumonia, hospital acquired or ventilator associated: IV: 2.5 gm every 8 hours; treatment is typically given for 7 days. ●Urinary tract infection: IV: 2.5 gm every 8 hours. Total duration of therapy ranges from 5 to 14 days. Pediatric: ●Intra-abdominal infection, complicated (cIAI): Use in combination with metronidazole. 5 to 14 days depending upon severity and clinical response. Infants <3 months: IV: 30 mg ceftazidime/kg/dose every 8 hours. Infants >=3 to <6 months: IV: 40 mg ceftazidime/kg/dose every 8 hours. Infants >=6 months, Children, and Adolescents <18 years: IV: 50 mg ceftazidime/kg/dose every 8 hours; maximum dose: 2,000 mg ceftazidime/dose. Adolescents >=18 years: 2,000 mg ceftazidime every 8 hours. ●Urinary tract infection, complicated (cUTI) (including pyelonephritis)/Pneumonia, hospital acquired or ventilator associated (HAP/VAP): 5 to 14 days depending upon severity and clinical response. Infants <3 months: IV: 30 mg ceftazidime/kg/dose every 8 hours. Infants >=3 to <6 months: IV: 40 mg ceftazidime/kg/dose every 8 hours. Infants >=6 months, Children, and Adolescents <18 years: IV: 50 mg ceftazidime/kg/dose every 8 hours; maximum dose: 2,000 mg ceftazidime/dose. Adolescents >=18 years: 2,000 mg ceftazidime every 8 hours. |
