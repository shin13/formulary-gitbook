| Dosing             | ●Adult Bloodstream infection: IV: 8 to 10 mg/kg once daily. Treat uncomplicated S. aureus bacteremia for ?14 days from day of first negative blood culture, with longer courses warranted for endocarditis or metastatic sites of infection. Endocarditis: Native valve: IV: 8 to 10 mg/kg once daily for 4 to 6 weeks; some experts use as part of an appropriate combination regimen. Prosthetic valve: IV: 10 mg/kg once daily for ?6 weeks as part of an appropriate combination regimen. Skin and skin structure infection: IV: 4 to 6 mg/kg once daily. Total duration of therapy is usually 5 to 14 days (including oral step-down therapy); for necrotizing infection, continue until further debridement is not necessary, patient has improved clinically, and patient is afebrile for >=48 hours. ●Pediatric Bacteremia: Children <7 years: IV: 12 mg/kg/dose every 24 hours. Children 7 to <12 years: IV: 9 mg/kg/dose every 24 hours. Children >=12 years and Adolescents <18 years: IV: 7 mg/kg/dose every 24 hours. Adolescents >=18 years: IV: 6 mg/kg/dose every 24 hours. Duration: Typically >=14 days is recommended for S. aureus bacteremia; may be variable based on source of bacteremia and clinical response. In trials evaluating daptomycin, total treatment duration (IV and oral step-down therapy) was 5 to 28 days in patients <12 years of age and 5 to 42 days in patients 12 to 17 years of age. Endocarditis: Children <6 years: IV: 10 to 12 mg/kg/dose every 24 hours. Children >=6 years and Adolescents: IV: 8 to 12 mg/kg/dose every 24 hours. Skin and soft tissue infection: Children <2 years: IV: 10 mg/kg/dose every 24 hours. Children 2 to <7 years: IV: 9 mg/kg dose every 24 hours. Children 7 to <12 years: IV: 7 mg/kg/dose every 24 hours. Children >=12 years and Adolescents <18 years: IV: 5 mg/kg/dose every 24 hours. Adolescents >=18 years: IV: 4 mg/kg/dose every 24 hours. Duration: The manufacturer recommends up to 14 days for complicated infection; in clinical trials, the median duration of IV therapy was 3 days (range: 1 to 10 days) and the median duration of total therapy (IV plus oral step-down therapy) was 12 days (range: 1 to 35 days).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
