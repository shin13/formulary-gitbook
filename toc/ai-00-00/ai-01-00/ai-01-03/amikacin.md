| Indications        | CNS infection, health care associated Cystic fibrosis, acute pulmonary exacerbation Endophthalmitis, bacterial Mycobacterial infection Nocardiosis, severe Peritonitis, treatment Pneumonia, hospital acquired or ventilator associated Sepsis or septic shock Tuberculosis, drug resistant Urinary tract infection                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Dosing             | Conventional/traditional dosing: IM, IV: 5 mg/kg every 8 hours or 7.5 mg/kg every 12 hours. High-dose, extended-interval dosing: IV: 15 to 20 mg/kg once daily; use with caution in patients with CrCl <40 mL/minute. Some data suggest doses up to 30 mg/kg once daily in patients who are critically ill. CNS infection, health care associated (eg, bacterial meningitis, cerebrospinal fluid shunt infection): IV (alternative agent): 5 mg/kg every 8 hours in combination with other appropriate agents. Pneumonia, hospital acquired or ventilator associated (alternative agent): IV: 15 to 20 mg/kg once daily as part of an appropriate combination regimen. Inhalation for nebulization (using injectable solution): 400 to 500 mg every 12 hours in combination with IV antimicrobial therapy. Sepsis or septic shock: IV: 15 to 30 mg/kg once daily as part of an appropriate combination regimen. Urinary tract infection (alternative agent): IV: 15 mg/kg once. |
