# Ensitrelvir

## Xocova 125mg (公費)

| TAH Drug Code      | [OXOC](https://www.tahsda.org.tw/drugs/hissearch.php?drug_code=OXOC)                                                                                                                                                  |
|:-------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Indications        | Indicated for the treatment of COVID-19 infection in adults and children aged 12 years and older without respiratory failure.                                                                                         |
| Dosing             | The usual dose for children aged 12 years and older and adults is 375 mg of ensitrelvir (3 Xocova tablets) orally on Day 1, followed by 125 mg of ensitrelvir (1 Xocova tablet) orally once daily on Days 2 to 5.     |
| Hepatic Impairment | Dose adjustment not necessary                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Renal Impairment   | Dose adjustment not necessary                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Contraindications  | Patients with a history of hypersensitivity to any component of this drug. Patients taking colchicine who have renal or hepatic impairment. Women who are pregnant or may become pregnant.                            |
| Adverse Effects    | Decreased HDL cholesterol, increased triglycerides, increased bilirubin, decreased blood cholesterol, rash, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, abdominal discomfort, headache, dyslipidemia, increased serum iron, pruritus. |
| Pregnancy          | Unknown                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Lactation          | Unknown                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| More Info          | [UpToDate](https://www.uptodate.com/contents/ensitrelvir-drug-information)                                                                                                                                            |
