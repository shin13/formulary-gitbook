# Lemborexant

## Dayvigo 5mg

| TAH Drug Code      | [ODAY](https://www.tahsda.org.tw/drugs/hissearch.php?drug_code=ODAY)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
|:-------------------|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Indications        | Insomnia, sleep onset or sleep maintenance                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Dosing             | Insomnia, sleep onset or sleep maintenance: Oral: 5 mg once daily, as needed, at bedtime with at least 7 hours before planned time of awakening; may increase to 10 mg based on response and tolerability; maximum dose: 10 mg/day.                                                                                             |
| Hepatic Impairment | Dose adjustment required                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Renal Impairment   | Dose adjustment not necessary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Contraindications  | Narcolepsy.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Adverse Effects    | 1% to 10%: Nervous system: Abnormal dreams (<=2%), drowsiness (7% to 10%), fatigue (7% to 10%), headache (5% to 6%), nightmares (<=2%), sleep paralysis (1% to 2%) <1%: Nervous system: Complex sleep-related disorder, hypnogenic hallucinations Frequency not defined: Cardiovascular: Palpitations Nervous system: Cataplexy |
| Pregnancy          | Unknown                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Lactation          | Unknown                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
