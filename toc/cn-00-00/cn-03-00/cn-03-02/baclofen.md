| Dosing             | Adult Muscle spasm and/or musculoskeletal pain: Initial: 5 to 10 mg 3 times daily as needed. Spasticity: Initial: 5 mg 1 to 3 times daily; may increase by 5 mg per dose every 3 days based on response and tolerability; usual maximum dose: 80 mg/day in divided doses. Some patients may require doses up to 120 mg/day. Pediatric Spasticity: Infants ?4 months and Children <2 years: Very limited data available: Oral: Usual dose: 10 to 20 mg/day in divided doses every 8 hours. Maximum daily dose: 40 mg/day. Children 2 to 7 years: Limited data available: Oral: Initial: 2.5 mg 3 times daily. Maximum daily dose: 60 mg/day. Children ?8 years and Adolescents: Limited data available in children <12 years: Oral: Initial: 5 mg 3 times daily. Maximum daily dose: 80 mg/day.
