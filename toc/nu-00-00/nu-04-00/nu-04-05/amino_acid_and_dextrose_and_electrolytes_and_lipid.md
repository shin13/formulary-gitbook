# Amino Acid + Dextrose + Electrolytes + Lipid

## PeriOlimel N4E emulsion 1500mL

| TAH Drug Code      | [IPERI](https://www.tahsda.org.tw/drugs/hissearch.php?drug_code=IPERI)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
|:-------------------|:---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Indications        | Indicated for parenteral nutrition in adults and children over 2 years of age who cannot or should not be fed orally or enterally due to contraindications.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Dosing             | ‧ 每 kg 體重 0.16 至 0.35 g 氮（1 至 2 g 的胺基酸/kg）,取決於病人的營養狀態及分解代謝壓力 (catabolic stress) 程度； ‧ 20 至 40 kcal/kg； ‧ 20 至 40 mL 液體 /kg,或者每一消耗的 kcal 需 1 至 1.5 mL。                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Hepatic Impairment | Dose adjustment not necessary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Renal Impairment   | Dose adjustment not necessary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Contraindications  | * Use in preterm infants, infants, and children under 2 years of age * Known allergy to egg, soy, peanut proteins, corn/corn products, any active substance, or excipients * Congenital amino acid metabolism disorders * Severe hyperlipidemia or severe lipid metabolism disorders (e.g., marked hypertriglyceridemia) * Severe hyperglycemia * Pathologically elevated plasma levels of sodium, potassium, magnesium, calcium, and/or phosphate |
| Adverse Effects    | Tachycardia, loss of appetite, hypertriglyceridemia, abdominal pain, diarrhea, nausea, hypertension, vomiting, allergic reactions including sweating, fever, chills, headache, and rash.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Pregnancy          | Unknown                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Lactation          | Unknown                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
