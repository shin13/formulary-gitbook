# Linagliptin + Metformin

## Trajenta Duo 2.5/850mg

| TAH Drug Code      | [OTRDU](https://www.tahsda.org.tw/drugs/hissearch.php?drug_code=OTRDU)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|:-------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Indications        | Diabetes mellitus, type 2, treatment                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Dosing             | Initial dosage: Patients taking metformin: Oral: Linagliptin 5 mg per day plus current daily dose of metformin administered in 2 divided daily doses (immediate release) or once daily (extended release). Patients not taking metformin: Oral: Linagliptin 5 mg/metformin 1 g per day administered in 2 divided daily doses (immediate release) or once daily (extended release). Dosage adjustment: May adjust metformin component gradually if needed to achieve glycemic targets (maximum: linagliptin 5 mg/metformin 2 g per day). |
| Hepatic Impairment | Dose adjustment required                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Renal Impairment   | Dose adjustment required                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Contraindications  | Hypersensitivity (eg, anaphylaxis, angioedema, exfoliative skin conditions, urticaria, bronchial hyperreactivity) to linagliptin, metformin, or any component of the formulation; severe renal impairment (eGFR <30 mL/minute/1.73 m2); acute or chronic metabolic acidosis, including diabetic ketoacidosis.                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Adverse Effects    | 1% to 10%: Gastrointestinal: Diarrhea (6%) Respiratory: Nasopharyngitis (6%) Frequency not defined: Dermatologic: Pruritus Endocrine & metabolic: Lactic acidosis Gastrointestinal: Decreased appetite, nausea, pancreatitis, vomiting Hypersensitivity: Hypersensitivity reaction (including angioedema) Respiratory: Cough Postmarketing: Neuromuscular & skeletal: Severe arthralgia                                                                                                                                                 |
| Pregnancy          | Human Data Suggest Low Risk                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Lactation          | No (Limited) Human Data - Probably Compatible                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
