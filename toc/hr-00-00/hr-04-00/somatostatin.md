# Somatostatin

## Stilamin inj 3mg

| TAH Drug Code      | [ISTI3](https://www.tahsda.org.tw/drugs/hissearch.php?drug_code=ISTI3)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
|:-------------------|:---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Indications        | Esophageal varices bleeding Intestinal, biliary, or pancreatic fistulas Pancreatic surgery complications, prophylaxis Upper GI bleeding, nonvariceal                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Dosing             | Esophageal varices bleeding: IV: Initial: 250 mcg bolus administered over 3 to 5 minutes, immediately followed by 250 to 500 mcg/hour as a continuous infusion for 2 to 5 days; may repeat initial bolus in first hour if hemorrhage not controlled. Intestinal, biliary, or pancreatic fistulas: IV: 250 mcg/hour as a continuous infusion. Continue until fistula closure, then decrease to 125 mcg/hour for 48 hours to avoid recurrence. Pancreatic surgery complications, prophylaxis: IV: 250 mcg/hour as a continuous infusion starting prior to surgery and continued for 5 days. Upper GI bleeding, nonvariceal: IV: Initial: 250 mcg bolus administered over 3 to 5 minutes, immediately followed by 250 mcg/hour as continuous infusion. Continue 48 to 72 hours after bleeding has stopped to avoid rebleeding; treatment duration up to 120 hours has been studied. |
